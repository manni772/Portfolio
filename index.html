About me

I'm a Master of Business Analysis student at Victoria University of Wellington, with a focus on the intersection of AI, policy, and data.

I taught myself to build real, working software through vibe coding — using AI as a collaborative development partner to create tools that solve genuine problems. Every project in my portfolio started as an idea and became a live product.

I'm particularly interested in how AI and technology can improve public sector decision-making — making policy analysis faster, more rigorous, and more accessible. The NZ Policy Research Assistant is a direct expression of that interest.

I'm looking for graduate roles where I can combine analytical rigour with technical capability — especially in policy, data, or product roles at organisations working on meaningful problems for Aotearoa.
